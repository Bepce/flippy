Your PDF catalogue, as a book on your own site

flippy turns a printed catalogue into pages that turn like paper. Every product code in it opens that product in your shop, and because it runs on your domain, the visitor’s cart keeps working.

From PDF to flipbook

  1. Open your PDF in the builder

    It runs in your browser. The PDF isn’t uploaded anywhere while you work.

  2. Link the product codes

    Give it a list of codes and addresses, your shop’s product feed, or one address pattern. It finds the codes on every page and draws a box around each colour. Move or resize any box by hand.

  3. Publish

    Send it to your flippy server in one click, or download the folder and upload it to any website.

Why it has to be your domain

Hosted flipbook services show your catalogue in a frame that belongs to their website. Browsers treat your shop inside that frame as a stranger and drop its cookies, so the cart empties and the visitor is signed out the moment they open a product.

flippy runs on your site. Products open over the catalogue with your shop’s own cookies, so whatever a visitor adds to the cart is still there when they check out. Your statistics stay yours too: views, pages and clicks, counted without cookies.

Self-host or hosted

Run it yourself

Free under the MIT licence. Put the viewer on any page with two lines:

<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Bepce/flippy@v0.4.0/dist/flippy.min.js"></script>
<flip-book src="/catalogues/spring/"></flip-book>

For a library of catalogues with sign-in and statistics, run the flippy server on PHP hosting or with Docker:

docker run -d -p 8080:80 -v flippy-data:/data ghcr.io/bepce/flippy-server

Read the setup guide on GitHub

Hosted for you

We run flippy for you at yourshop.flippy.io: your catalogue library, the builder, statistics, backups and updates. You paste one line into your shop, and your catalogues appear there.

The cart keeps working, because the viewer still runs inside your shop’s pages.
