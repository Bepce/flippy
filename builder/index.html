flippy builder

Turn a PDF catalogue into a flipbook with product links

Drop in your catalogue. The builder finds the product codes printed on the pages and links each one to your shop. Adjust the boxes by hand, preview the flipbook, and export a folder to upload to your website.

Everything happens in this browser tab. Your PDF isn't uploaded anywhere.
